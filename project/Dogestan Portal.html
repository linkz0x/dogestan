The Founding Document

The Constitution of Dogestan

❦  ·  ❦  ·  ❦
THE CONSTITUTION
of the Sovereign Republic of Dogestan · Ratified at Block 18,000,000

We, the holders of the Dogestan token, in order to form a more liquid union, establish a sovereignty unencumbered by geography, promote the general up-only, and secure the blessings of yield to ourselves and our diamond-handed posterity, do ordain and establish this Constitution for the Sovereign Republic of Dogestan.

IOF SOVEREIGNTY

The Republic exists where the contract is verified.

The Sovereign Republic of Dogestan shall consist of all addresses holding, at any block height, one (1) or more units of the national token. The Republic has no physical territory, requires none, and shall not, under any administration, acquire any. A flagpole may be erected on Block 21,000,000 ceremonially.

IIOF CITIZENSHIP

To hold the token is to be a citizen.

Citizenship in the Republic is conferred automatically and irrevocably upon the receipt of any non-zero balance of the national token. Citizenship may be transferred to a third party at the citizen’s discretion. Dual citizenship with other nations — including those with land — is expressly permitted and quietly encouraged.

IIIOF THE TREASURY

The Treasury shall be public, on-chain, and immutable.

All assets of the Republic shall be held in a multi-signature wallet, the signatories to which are the President, the Minister of the Treasury, and a randomly-selected citizen drawn each quarter. Any disbursement shall be subject to a public vote of no fewer than seventy-two (72) hours. The Treasury may not be denominated in fiat currencies of any nation that has, in the last decade, defaulted on its sovereign debt.

IVOF THE PRESIDENCY

The Head of State serves at the pleasure of liquidity.

The President shall hold office for so long as the Republic maintains a market capitalisation in excess of five hundred thousand United States dollars ($500,000). Should the Republic fall below this threshold for fourteen (14) consecutive days, the office shall pass to the largest remaining holder, who may not refuse.

VOF FOREIGN RELATIONS

We extend recognition to all nations, with or without land.

The Republic shall maintain peaceful and respectful relations with all sovereign states, whether constituted of soil, of charter, or of contract address. The Republic shall not engage in war, having no army; nor in trade tariffs, having no border; nor in diplomatic intrigue, having no ambassador. Memes may, however, be exchanged at par.

VIOF AMENDMENT

The Constitution may be amended by supermajority of token holders.

Any amendment to this Constitution shall require the affirmative vote of two-thirds (²⁄₃) of the supply, measured by token weight, over a period of no fewer than seven (7) days. The text of this Constitution, once ratified, shall be inscribed on-chain in calldata, and shall not be removable while a single node remains.

Department of Citizenship — Enrolment

Becoming a Citizen of Dogestan

Citizenship is conferred by the act of holding the national token. No paperwork is required. No oath is administered. The chain is the registrar.

Form DG-001 · Notice of Enrolment

OFFICIAL ENROLMENT INSTRUCTIONS

Issued under authority of the Department of Citizenship.
  1. Connect a sovereign wallet.
    A non-custodial wallet on the Ethereum mainnet is required. Hardware wallets are recognised as “reinforced sovereign instruments”.
  2. Acquire one (1) or more units of the national token.
    Any non-zero balance shall be sufficient. Fractional holdings are recognised as full citizenship for the purposes of voting and bragging.
  3. Await on-chain confirmation.
    Citizenship is conferred at the moment the transaction reaches finality. No further filings are required.
  4. Optionally, declare yourself.
    Citizens are invited — but not required — to update their on-chain profile, social handle, or telegram bio to reflect their new nationality.
